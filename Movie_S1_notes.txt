The maps contain integer region IDs. Their positive-label support is converted

Surface smoothing is unchanged from the accepted earlier movies: 50 iterations
with a factor of 0.3, using adjacent-vertex averaging. Surrounding membrane
surface models are absent. The grayscale tomogram naturally shows membrane

REPRODUCIBILITY
The delivered MP4 was rendered directly from the supplied MRC data with
VTK 9.5.2 and OSMesa. It is a data-derived scientific visualization. The
original CXS session and numeric camera settings were not supplied; the
camera sequence is reconstructed for the three requested highlights.

Movie_S1_render_vtk.py is the renderer used for this MP4. Place the five MRCs
beside the script or in input/ or inputs/. The script prefers capsid filenames
with (1) and also accepts the explicit names without that suffix. It requires
NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, VTK 9.5+ with vtkOSOpenGLRenderWindow, an OSMesa runtime,
and ffmpeg on PATH. Run:
    python Movie_S1_render_vtk.py --movie
Use --preview for a storyboard. Output goes to rendered/ by default.
HERVK_PROJECT_DIR, HERVK_INPUT_DIR, and HERVK_OUTPUT_DIR can choose working,
input, and output folders. HERVK_VTK_PATH can select a private VTK installation.

Movie_S1_native_setup.py provides the companion native ChimeraX route; see
README.txt. It prepares the scene, generates a recording script, and can save
a CXS session and movie when run in ChimeraX. The companion was syntax-checked
and its generated frame count verified; it has not been executed in ChimeraX
here. Its native lighting is a visual counterpart to the rendered review.

Movie_S1_render_settings.json records the exact parameters. Four PNG previews
show the overview and the three highlights. The original MRC inputs are not
duplicated in the scripts archive.
